Foundations

PayFlow Design System

The visual source of truth for the PayFlow payroll SaaS. Style direction follows current Dribbble research (see DESIGN.md): calm, data-dense-but-breathable SaaS surfaces; one signature accent carrying the product; tabular money figures; elevation through tonal steps, not heavy borders.

Calm & trustworthy

Fintech must feel safe. Quiet neutrals, restrained indigo, teal reserved for money-positive moments.

Numbers first

Payroll is numbers. Hero KPI typography, tabular-nums everywhere money appears, right-aligned columns.

Role-aware

Status pills, role badges and workspace banners make system state legible at a glance.

Never dead-ended

Every screen designs its loading, empty, error, forbidden and read-only states as first-class citizens.

Foundations

Color

Indigo is the product voice (actions, links, brand). Teal is money/positive financial signal only — never for decoration. Slate carries all text and structure.

Dashboard pattern

Bento composition: hero KPI + three quiet KPIs, primary trend chart with dashed previous-period comparison, then role-relevant modules (onboarding checklist, pending approvals, recent payments). Charts use gradient area fills, rounded strokes, faint single-y gridlines only.
